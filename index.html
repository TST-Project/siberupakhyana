The story of King Śibi

in the Vahnipurāṇa & the Itihāsasamuccaya

A critical edition & translation of the śiber upākhyāna

Charles Li

This digital edition began as the supplementary material to the article King Śibi in the Vahnipurāṇa, published in Asian Literature and Translation 10(1) (doi:10.18573/alt.58). That article contained a critical edition and translation of the śiber upākhyāna in the Vahnipurāṇa. However, after discovring that the text was almost identical to the version contained in the Itihāsasamuccaya, this project was enlarged to contain two critical editions, one of each version, as well as a hypertext translation connecting the Vahnipurāṇa, Itihāsasamuccaya, Mahābhārata, Vikramacarita, as well as many of the other texts that share material with these stories. Apart from the critical texts, diplomatic transcriptions of each witness are also included, linked to images of the source material where available.
